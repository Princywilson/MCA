## **Experiment No: 2**

## **Title: Creating and Editing Various Video Clippings and Adding Special Effects Using Adobe Premiere Pro**

---

## **Aim:**

To learn and perform basic and advanced video editing techniques by creating and editing various video clippings, arranging them on a timeline, trimming and merging clips, adding transitions, special effects, background music, titles, and exporting the final edited video using Adobe Premiere Pro.

---

## **Requirements:**

* Computer system with minimum 8GB RAM
* Adobe Premiere Pro installed
* Sample video clips
* Audio files (background music or voice-over)
* Image files (if required for slideshow or overlay)

---

## **Procedure:**

### **Step 1: Launching the Software**

1. Start the computer system.
2. Open Adobe Premiere Pro from the Start menu or desktop shortcut.
3. Click on **New Project**.
4. Enter the project name and choose the project location.
5. Click **OK** to create the project workspace.

---

### **Step 2: Importing Media Files**

1. Go to the **File** menu and select **Import**.
2. Choose the required video clips, images, and audio files from the storage location.
3. Click **Open**.
4. The imported files will appear in the **Project Panel**.

---

### **Step 3: Creating a Sequence**

1. Drag a video clip into the **Timeline Panel**.
2. The software automatically creates a sequence matching the clip settings.
3. Rename the sequence if necessary.

---

### **Step 4: Editing and Trimming Clips**

1. Use the **Selection Tool** to arrange clips on the timeline.
2. Use the **Razor Tool** to cut unwanted parts.
3. Trim the beginning or ending of clips by dragging the clip edges.
4. Remove unnecessary segments by selecting and pressing **Delete**.
5. Adjust clip duration and positioning properly.

---

### **Step 5: Adding Transitions**

1. Open the **Effects Panel**.
2. Select **Video Transitions**.
3. Drag and drop transitions like **Cross Dissolve**, Fade, Slide, etc., between two clips.
4. Adjust the transition duration by dragging its edges in the timeline.

Transitions help in making the video smooth and professional.

---

### **Step 6: Adding Special Effects**

1. In the **Effects Panel**, select **Video Effects**.
2. Apply effects such as:

   * Blur
   * Color Correction
   * Brightness & Contrast
   * Black & White
3. Drag the desired effect onto the selected clip.
4. Modify effect properties in the **Effect Controls Panel**.

Special effects enhance the visual quality and appearance of the video.

---

### **Step 7: Adding Text and Titles**

1. Select the **Type Tool**.
2. Click on the Program Monitor and type the required text.
3. Adjust font size, color, and alignment.
4. Position the text properly on the screen.
5. Add opening titles and closing credits.

---

### **Step 8: Adding Audio**

1. Drag the audio file to the **Audio Track** in the timeline.
2. Adjust the audio length to match the video.
3. Control volume using the **Audio Gain** option.
4. Add fade-in and fade-out effects if required.

---

### **Step 9: Previewing the Project**

1. Press the **Spacebar** to preview the video.
2. Check for synchronization between audio and video.
3. Make necessary corrections if required.

---

### **Step 10: Exporting the Final Video**

1. Click on **File → Export → Media**.
2. Choose format as **H.264**.
3. Select preset (e.g., High Quality 1080p).
4. Choose output location.
5. Click **Export**.

The final video is saved in the selected folder.

---

## **Result:**

Thus, various video clips were successfully imported, arranged, trimmed, and edited using Adobe Premiere Pro. Special effects, transitions, text titles, and background audio were added effectively. The final edited video was exported successfully in the desired format with enhanced visual and audio quality.

---

## **Output:**

The output of this experiment is a professionally edited video containing:

* Multiple merged video clips
* Smooth transitions between scenes
* Applied special effects
* Background music synchronized with visuals
* Opening titles and end credits
* Proper color correction and audio adjustment

The exported video file was saved in MP4 format and played successfully without any errors.
